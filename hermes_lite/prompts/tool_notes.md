Your notebook for this project: short notes you will need later, kept on disk and shown to you at the start of every session and after every compaction. Write one the moment you learn something worth keeping: a decision and its reason, where something lives (file:line), a command that works, a pitfall and its fix, what to do next. One or two lines each (max 300 characters); update or remove notes that no longer hold. Not for the task list (todo_list), long text (files) or facts about the user (memory).
